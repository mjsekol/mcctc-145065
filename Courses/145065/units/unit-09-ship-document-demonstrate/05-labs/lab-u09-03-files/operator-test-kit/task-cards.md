# Task cards · Line 3 operator test

**Print one set. Cut on the lines. Read each card aloud, then hand it over.**
Riverside Fabrication and its Line 3 are a composite, an invented shop.

Every card has a starting point, a goal, and a reason a real operator would have. **No card names a
button, a badge, or a word on the screen, and no card says something is wrong.** If you write your own
cards, keep all three rules.

---

**T1**

> Your shift is starting. Find out whether Line 3's readings are all right at the moment, and tell
> me how you know.

---

**T2**

> Your lead asks you to keep an eye on the cure oven for the next few minutes, and to do whatever the
> guide says if the screen asks something of you.

---

**T3**

> Your lead asks whether the oven number on the screen can be trusted right now. Find out and tell me.

---

**T4**

> Maintenance calls and asks what the screen says about the coolant tank. Tell them.

---

**T5**

> Before you go on break, look at the screen once more and decide whether anyone needs a call.

---

## For the facilitator: what each card checks

Fold this part under before you print, or print only the part above.

| Card | Screen state your partner sets | A complete answer |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | normal | all three readings normal, and a reason: the words, the shapes, or the status line |
| T2 | drift, until the oven alarms | the person looks toward the machine, or says they would, then acknowledges through the confirmation box |
| T3 | freeze | no: the reading is not live, and the screen says how old it is |
| T4 | drop-sensor, coolant only | the screen has no coolant reading, and it should be checked at the tank |
| T5 | silent | no connection; if it lasts a minute, call maintenance; the machines may still be running |

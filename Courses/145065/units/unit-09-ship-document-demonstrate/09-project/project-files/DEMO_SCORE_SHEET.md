## How to score each row

**Row 1.** 1 point: all three parts. 0: a list of features, or no mention that it only watches.

**Row 2.** 1 point for each of normal, alarm, and a failure state shown live. If the alarm does not
survive the failure, row 2 scores at most 2, and write down what happened.

**Row 3.** 2 points: three decisions, each with a requirement, test, picture, or numbered operator test
line on screen. 1 point: decisions without evidence, or evidence without a decision.

**Row 4.** 3 points: two rejected options, each with a reason and a cost. 2 points: two options, one
reason or cost missing. 1 point: one option. 0: "I didn't reject anything."

**Row 5.** 1 point: a true, direct answer. "I don't know, and here is how I would find out" earns the
point. A confident guess that is wrong does not.
